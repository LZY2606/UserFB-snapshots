ogg_mux_submit_page

declared in "ogg/mux.h";

Hands one complete page, already framed and checksummed by the regular encoding layer, to the multiplexer. On success the page is copied and the caller's buffer may be reused at once. On failure nothing is queued and the instance's state, including watermarks and anchors, is unchanged.

A track's pages must arrive in order: its first page must be the BOS page numbered 0, followed by consecutive page numbers, ending with an EOS page. A non-BOS page with granule −1 is grouped with the next page of the same track that has a non-negative granule. A track may not have two open groups.

The page size counts against the instance's byte limit. If it does not fit, OGG_MUX_CAPACITY is returned without consuming input; after ogg_mux_set_limit raises the limit the identical page may be resubmitted. 


ogg_mux_result_t ogg_mux_submit_page(ogg_mux_t *mux,
                                     const ogg_page *page);

Parameters

mux
The multiplexer instance.
page
A complete page whose serial belongs to a registered track.

Return Values

  • OGG_MUX_OK: accepted and copied.
  • OGG_MUX_CAPACITY: the byte limit would be exceeded.
  • OGG_MUX_NEED_BOS: a non-BOS page arrived before this track's BOS.
  • OGG_MUX_BAD_SERIAL: the page's serial is not registered.
  • OGG_MUX_BAD_PAGE: malformed header, duplicate BOS, wrong page number, or a page after EOS.
  • OGG_MUX_BAD_GRANULE: a negative granule other than −1, an EOS page without a non-negative granule, or an anchor that moves backwards.
  • OGG_MUX_FRONTIER: the page does not schedule strictly after its track's committed watermark.
  • OGG_MUX_INVALID: NULL argument, or input already ended.
  • OGG_MUX_NOMEM: allocation failure.
